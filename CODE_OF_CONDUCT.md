# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and considerate. Welcome contributors across languages and experience levels. Discuss code and evidence without personal attacks. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and publication of private credentials are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. Report sensitive conduct concerns through the repository's private security-reporting channel when available; do not publish personal information. Decisions should protect participant safety and explain the relevant rule where practical. This policy applies to this project's issues, discussions and pull requests; it does not claim authority over unrelated communities.
